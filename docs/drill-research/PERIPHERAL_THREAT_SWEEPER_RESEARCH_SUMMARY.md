## 3. Scientific Foundations & Peer-Reviewed Literature
- **Posner (1980)**: *Orienting of attention* (Quarterly Journal of Experimental Psychology, DOI: `10.1080/00488408008415784`). Validates the spatial cueing paradigm and demonstrates that covert attentional shifts occur without eye movements to prime visual receptive fields.
- **Treisman & Gelade (1980)**: *A feature-integration theory of attention* (Cognitive Psychology, DOI: `10.1016/0010-0285(80)90005-5`). Validates parallel pre-attentive feature detection of high-contrast peripheral target nodes.
- **Woodworth (1899)**: *The accuracy of voluntary movement* (Psychological Review, DOI: `10.1037/h0092992`). Two-component motor model of open-loop ballistic radial snaps followed by closed-loop corrective interception.
- **Fitts (1954)**: *The information capacity of the human motor system in rapid aimed movements* (JEP, DOI: `10.1037/h0055392`). Speed-accuracy tradeoff under escalating radial target velocity and diminishing core radius.
- **Woods et al. (2015)**: *Age-related changes in visual reaction time* (Frontiers in Human Neuroscience, DOI: `10.3389/fnhum.2015.00131`). Mental chronometry baselines for multimodal motor reaction latency.
