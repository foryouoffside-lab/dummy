## 1. Executive Summary & Market Findings

The `physical` category covers motor sequencing, whole-body biomechanical rhythm, precision cursor stabilization, evasive reaction mechanics, and bilateral cross-midline coordination. 

Unlike pure FPS aim trainers (which compete directly against high-authority game hubs like Aimlabs or Human Benchmark), the **Physical category represents a massive high-demand, low-competition opportunity ("Soft SERP")** because:
1. **The "Ruler Drop" / "Drop Catch" Gap:** Search volume for `ruler drop test` / `drop catch reflex` is high globally across school, athletic, and physical therapy contexts, yet **almost 100% of top-ranking search results are static educational articles or YouTube videos**. No major competitor provides an instant, interactive HTML5 drop-catch reaction tool with millisecond precision and decoy discrimination.
2. **The "Peripheral Vision" Opportunity:** Searches like `peripheral vision test online` (US), `周辺視野 トレーニング ゲーム` (Japan), and `gesichtsfeld test online` (Germany) are actively suggested by Google Autocomplete, but the SERPs are dominated by medical clinic directories and static optometry PDFs. An interactive browser game provides immediate high utility.
3. **The "Mouse Dodging" Evasion Moat:** Korean (`마우스 피하기 게임`), Japanese (`障害物 回避 ゲーム`), and German (`ausweichspiel online`) searchers actively seek cursor dodging and evasion challenges. Since the death of Adobe Flash, legacy evasion games are broken across the web. Our responsive 144Hz HTML5 Canvas drills (`quick-dodge` and `dynamic-grid-evasion`) satisfy this dormant, high-intent query cluster.
