## 1. Overview & Search Architecture
Stroboscopic training conditions predictive visual tracking and motion extrapolation by rhythmically suppressing visual input through intermittent occlusions. Under stroboscopic blackout phases, the retina stops transmitting continuous visual sensory feedback, preventing the medial temporal area (MT/V5) and posterior parietal cortex from operating under standard closed-loop visual feedback. Instead, the central nervous system must engage cerebellar internal models to compute feedforward kinematic trajectory predictions—estimating velocity, inertia, and angular path. This drill simulates high-performance stroboscopic training glasses in a responsive browser canvas.
