## 1. Executive Summary
The **Peripheral Ping Pursuit** drill is a dual-task neurovisual protocol designed to develop covert spatial attention and expand the useful field of view (UFOV). Users sustain continuous foveal smooth pursuit tracking on a moving central reticle while simultaneously detecting transient luminance flashes ('pings') across peripheral quadrants without making disruptive saccades.

All 7 locales (English, German, Korean, Japanese, Portuguese, Spanish, and French) have been built with authentic domestic search keywords, 6 Schema.org JSON-LD microdata scripts, 10 bespoke scientific FAQs, and 5-tier scientific benchmark tables.
