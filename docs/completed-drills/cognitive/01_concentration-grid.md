## 1. Overview & Search Architecture
The Schulte Table / Concentration Grid is a classical psychodiagnostic and cognitive conditioning instrument designed to widen the Functional Field of View (FFOV) and minimize fixation latency during sequential visual search. When finding ordered numerical targets on expanding dynamic grids (3x3 up to 8x8) under a strict 45-second window, visual crowding increases. Skilled performers minimize central foveal re-orientations, using parafoveal perception to process neighboring numbers and plan anticipatory saccades without regressions.
