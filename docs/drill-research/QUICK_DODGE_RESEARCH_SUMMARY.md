## 3. Scientific Foundations & Peer-Reviewed Literature
- **Mitsuo Kawato (1999)**: *Internal models for motor control and trajectory planning* (Current Opinion in Neurobiology, DOI: `10.1016/S0959-4388(99)00028-8`). Establishes cerebellar forward models that compute predictive motor commands to avoid collision trajectories before visual feedback latency elapses.
- **Robert Woodworth (1899)**: *The accuracy of voluntary movement* (Psychological Review, DOI: `10.1037/h0092992`). Two-component motor model of open-loop ballistic radial snaps followed by closed-loop corrective stabilization in safe corridors.
- **Paul Fitts (1954)**: *The information capacity of the human motor system in rapid aimed movements* (JEP, DOI: `10.1037/h0055392`). Fitts's Law governing the speed-accuracy tradeoff under constricting spatial boundaries and accelerating obstacle velocities.
- **David N. Lee (1976)**: *A theory of visual control of braking based on information about time-to-collision* (Perception, DOI: `10.1068/p050437`). Optical tau ($\tau$) margin calculation for predicting time-to-contact of convergent homing threats.
- **Woods et al. (2015)**: *Factors influencing the latency of simple reaction time* (Frontiers in Human Neuroscience, DOI: `10.3389/fnhum.2015.00131`). Mental chronometry baselines for multimodal motor reaction latency.
