## 1. Overview & Search Architecture
The Multitasking Test (Dual-Target Flow Drill) evaluates bilateral hemispheric visual tracking, cognitive flexibility, and task switching efficiency under continuous speed pressure. Operating with two independent geometric shape streams traveling in opposing directions, the drill tests the prefrontal cortex's executive control network to sustain rapid alternation without suffering severe task-switching performance degradation (Rogers & Monsell, 1995). Cognitive neuroscience proves that complex, non-automated operations do not perform true simultaneous parallel computation; rather, the human brain executes rapid time-sliced serial processing governed by a central bottleneck (Pashler, 1994; Wickens, 2002). Habitual multitaskers often suffer increased vulnerability to visual distraction (Ophir, Nass & Wagner, 2009), making structured dual-stream calibration essential for optimizing mental throughput in esports, aviation, and high-load professional environments.
