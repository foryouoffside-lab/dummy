## 1. Overview & Search Architecture
The Divided Attention Test (Dual-Task Paradigm) evaluates the central executive network's ability to allocate attentional resources across simultaneous, non-congruent cognitive tasks without excessive performance degradation. According to the central capacity sharing model and psychological refractory period (PRP) theory (Pashler, 1994; Wickens, 2002), executing a continuous visual tracking task alongside a rapid serial visual presentation (RSVP) numerical classification forces the central bottleneck to rapidly switch temporal resources. In ecological environments such as esports, high-speed driving, and aviation, divided attention efficiency directly predicts accident avoidance and rapid operational adaptation (Strayer & Johnston, 2001; Spelke et al., 1976).
