## 1. Executive Summary
The **Predictive Pursuit** drill conditions internal cerebellar forward models and feedforward smooth pursuit tracking across temporary visual occlusions. By requiring the user's oculomotor system to mentally extrapolate continuous velocity and trajectory vectors without relying on closed-loop sensory feedback, this exercise overcomes the biological 130–150 ms sensorimotor processing latency barrier.
