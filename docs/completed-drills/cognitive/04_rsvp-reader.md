## 1. Overview & Search Architecture
Rapid Serial Visual Presentation (RSVP) is an evidence-based cognitive experimental paradigm designed to eliminate the mechanical bottlenecks of human reading (Rayner, 1998; Rayner et al., 2016). In natural page reading, up to 80% of total time is spent executing saccadic eye movements (20–40ms each), settling during foveal fixations (200–250ms), and correcting for involuntary regressive saccades. By presenting words sequentially at a fixed central focal target and anchoring each term to its Optimal Recognition Point (ORP — slightly left of center), RSVP neutralizes oculomotor overhead, allowing visual word recognition and lexical access to scale smoothly up to 850 words per minute (WPM).
