## 3. Scientific Foundations & Peer-Reviewed Literature
- **Gordon D. Logan & William B. Cowan (1984)**: *On the ability to inhibit thought and action: A theory of an act of control* (Journal of Experimental Psychology: Human Perception and Performance, DOI: `10.1037/0096-1523.10.2.276`). Establishes the stop-signal horse-race model where voluntary stopping is an active inhibitory race between a GO process and a STOP process.
- **Robert Woodworth (1899)**: *The accuracy of voluntary movement* (Psychological Review, DOI: `10.1037/h0092992`). Two-component motor model: initial open-loop ballistic movement followed by closed-loop kinetic dampening and antagonist muscle clamping.
- **Paul Fitts (1954)**: *The information capacity of the human motor system in rapid aimed movements* (JEP, DOI: `10.1037/h0055392`). Speed-accuracy tradeoff under constricting target radii and high deceleration velocities.
- **Franciscus Donders (1868/1969)**: *On the speed of mental processes* (Acta Psychologica translation, DOI: `10.1016/0001-6918(69)90065-1`). Type C mental chronometry and discrimination pacing for selective motor arrest versus continuous movement.
- **Woods et al. (2015)**: *Factors influencing the latency of simple reaction time* (Frontiers in Human Neuroscience, DOI: `10.3389/fnhum.2015.00131`). Mental chronometry baselines for multimodal motor reaction latency.
