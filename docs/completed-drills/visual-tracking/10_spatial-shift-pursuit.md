## 1. Overview & Search Architecture
Spatial shift pursuit trains the posterior parietal cortex (PPC) and ocular motor nuclei to execute rapid coordinate transformations when reference frames translate or rotate unpredictably. Unlike static smooth pursuit, observers must integrate retinal slip with efference copy motor signals, execute high-velocity ballistic catch-up saccades (up to 500°/s), and immediately blend into continuous smooth pursuit along the new trajectory vector without hesitation (post-saccadic pursuit handshake).
