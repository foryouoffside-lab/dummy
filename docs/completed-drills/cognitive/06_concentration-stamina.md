## 1. Overview & Search Architecture
The Continuous Performance Test (CPT) and Concentration Stamina drill measure the neurobiological capacity to sustain continuous visual vigilance over extended time horizons while resisting attentional decay (Mackworth, 1948). During continuous visual monitoring, detection efficiency systematically degrades after 20 to 30 minutes as prefrontal cognitive resources and noradrenergic arousal deplete (Parasuraman, 1979; Robertson et al., 1997). To create an ecologically rigorous challenge, this drill introduces dynamic task-switching rules every 10 seconds (alternating between Vowels and Prime Numbers), forcing the prefrontal cortex to clear working memory buffers and actively inhibit automatic response habits (Monsell, 2003).
