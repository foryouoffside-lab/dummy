## 1. Executive Summary
The **Momentum Teleport Pursuit** drill trains rapid foveal re-acquisition following sudden spatial displacements (teleportation) while preserving linear velocity momentum. This exercises the neuromuscular handoff between ballistic saccadic re-targeting and feedforward smooth pursuit tracking.
